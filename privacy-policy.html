3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our Site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies. A cookie is a small file stored on your device that helps us understand how you use our Site.

Cookies We Use

  • Google Analytics (GA4): Tracks page views, session duration, traffic sources, and user behaviour. Data is anonymised. Google Privacy Policy

  • Session Storage: Used locally in your browser to remember if you have seen certain notifications (e.g. exit popup). This data never leaves your device.

You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. You can manage your Meta ad preferences at facebook.com/adpreferences.
