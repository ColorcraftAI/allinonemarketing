Cookie Policy

What cookies we use and how to control them

📅 Last updated: April 28, 2026

Summary: We use cookies from Google Analytics and Meta (Facebook) to understand how visitors use our Site and to run advertising. We do not use cookies to collect personal information. You can opt out at any time.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, phone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit — such as which pages you viewed — making your next visit easier and the site more useful to you.

Cookies cannot run programs or deliver viruses to your device. They are uniquely assigned to you and can only be read by the web server that issued them.

2. Cookies We Use

We use the following types of cookies on this Site:

Cookie Name Provider Purpose Duration
_ga, _ga_* Google Analytics Tracks page views, session duration, traffic sources, and user behaviour. Data is anonymised. 2 years
_fbp, _fbc Meta (Facebook) Used by Facebook Pixel to track ad performance, measure conversions, and build advertising audiences. 90 days
exit_seen AllinoneMarketing (Session Storage) Remembers if you have already seen our exit intent notification. Stored locally in your browser only — never sent to any server. Session only

3. Types of Cookies by Category

Analytics Cookies — Help us understand how visitors interact with our Site. We use Google Analytics (GA4) which collects anonymised data about page views, session duration, and traffic sources. This helps us improve our content.

Marketing Cookies — Used by Meta Pixel to track the effectiveness of our Facebook and Instagram advertising campaigns. These cookies allow us to show more relevant ads to people who have visited our Site and to measure how many people click our ads and visit the Site.

Functional Cookies (Session Storage) — We use your browser's Session Storage (not traditional cookies) to remember if you have seen our exit notification. This data is stored only on your device and is automatically cleared when you close your browser.

4. How to Control Cookies

You have several options to control or limit how cookies are used:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete existing cookies. Visit your browser's help section for instructions.
  • Google Analytics opt-out: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to prevent your data from being used by Google Analytics.
  • Meta ad preferences: Manage your Facebook ad settings at facebook.com/adpreferences.
  • Your Online Choices: Visit youronlinechoices.eu to opt out of interest-based advertising from multiple providers.
  • NAI opt-out: Visit optout.networkadvertising.org to opt out of network advertising.

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of this and other websites you visit.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our Site are set by third-party services. We do not control these cookies and they are subject to the privacy policies of their respective providers:

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta (Facebook): facebook.com/privacy/policy

6. GDPR & Cookie Consent

If you are visiting from the European Union or United Kingdom, you have the right to consent to or refuse non-essential cookies. By continuing to use this Site, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

We are working on implementing a cookie consent banner for EU/UK visitors. In the meantime, you can use the opt-out methods listed in Section 4 above.

7. Changes to This Policy
